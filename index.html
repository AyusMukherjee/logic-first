Pseudocode tips
  • Write one step per line. Indent the steps inside a loop or an if.
  • Name your data structures: "a set called seen", "a stack", "a map from value to index".
  • Be specific about edges: "for i from 0 to n − 1", "while left < right".
  • Say exactly what you return, including the "not found" case.
  • Plain English is fine. Claude follows what you wrote, not what you meant.
